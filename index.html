I am an Assistant Professor at the School of Artificial Intelligence at Shandong University. My research interests include Computer Vision, Video Understanding, Action Recognition, and Embodied Intelligence.
我是山东大学人工智能学院助理教授。我的研究方向包括计算机视觉、视频理解、行为分析和具身智能。

I received my Ph.D. degree in Computer Science from Shandong University in 2022, where I focused on deep learning-based human action recognition algorithms. Prior to that, I completed my M.S. degree at Shandong University in 2017, working on RGB-D based large-scale action recognition datasets.
我于2022年获得山东大学计算机科学博士学位，专注于基于深度学习的人体行为识别算法研究。2017年获得山东大学硕士学位，研究基于RGB-D大规模数据集的人体行为识别算法。
